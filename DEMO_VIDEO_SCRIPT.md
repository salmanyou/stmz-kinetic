# Your 60-second demo video — script & shot list

Record this in one take. **Total target: 50–60 seconds.** Use any free screen recorder (Loom, OBS, the built-in macOS Cmd+Shift+5, or Windows Game Bar). Voice over while you click, OR record silent and add captions in editing.

## Before you press record

- Open STMZ Kinetic in a clean browser window. Close every other tab.
- Pre-create a believable brand: name **"Sole Karachi"**, sells **"Handmade leather sandals, delivery across Pakistan"**, audience **"women 25–40 who care about ethical fashion"**, voice **"warm and conversational"**.
- Pre-generate ~5 posts so the Library doesn't look empty.
- Have one Pollinations image already loaded on screen (so it doesn't pop in mid-recording).
- Resolution: record at **1920×1080** (or your monitor's native). Crop later for vertical if needed.

## Honest framing notes for the script

- **Don't promise full-platform auto-posting.** Say "LinkedIn directly, others via Make.com." That's true and impressive — most schedulers work this way.
- **Don't fake numbers.** No "trusted by 500 businesses" if you don't have 500 businesses. Just show the product working.
- **Speak naturally.** Don't read this script word-for-word — paraphrase. Authentic > polished.
- **One take is fine.** Editors prefer authentic over perfect. A 5% mistake makes it feel real.
