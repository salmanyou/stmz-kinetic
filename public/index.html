FAQ

Honest answers.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is built for one-off chats. STMZ Kinetic is built for execution. It saves your brand context, stores every post you've made, organises content on a calendar, auto-posts at scheduled times, and supports a repeatable weekly workflow. ChatGPT gives you ideas; STMZ Kinetic gets them posted.

Does it post to my accounts automatically?

Yes. LinkedIn posts publish directly from your account once you connect (one-click OAuth, no third-party tools). For Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, WhatsApp Business and others, you connect a free Make.com or Zapier webhook once — STMZ Kinetic routes every scheduled post through it. We chose this hybrid architecture because direct OAuth into every platform requires months of vendor approvals; the webhook path works on day one and is how most leading schedulers integrate non-Meta-partner platforms.

What is AutoPilot?

Set up your brand and preferences once. AutoPilot generates your week of content every Monday automatically — using insights from your past top-performing posts to bias toward what works for your audience. You approve in one click (or skip approval and have posts auto-schedule). Available on Pro and Agency tiers.

Can agencies manage multiple clients?

Yes. The Agency tier allows unlimited brand profiles. Switch between clients in the sidebar — each has its own voice, library, calendar, AutoPilot settings and integrations. A single Agency seat replaces ChatGPT + Buffer + a freelancer for 5–20 clients.

What's actually free?

Demo mode (no sign-up required) gives you 3 AI batches per day, forever. You see the whole workspace. Library and calendar work locally. Subscribe only when you need unlimited generations, more brand profiles, or AutoPilot.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancellation is one click; you keep access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked, no retention sales calls.
